Help

How to use DyslexiLens with the mouse, the keyboard or your voice.

Reading a document

  1. Choose a PDF, JPG or PNG file and press Scan, or press Use sample text.
  2. Check the text. Fix any scanning mistakes, then press Read this text.
  3. Change the font size, spacing or background. Press Read whole text to listen.
  4. Click a tinted word to see its syllables and hear it slowly.
  5. Turn on One sentence at a time to read one sentence on its own. The app remembers where you stopped.

Voice commands

After logging in, press the Voice button at the bottom right or press V, then say one command. Say help to hear this list.

Logging in with your voice

On the login page, press Enter or click Start. The app asks if you want to use your voice. Say yes, then say your username. The app spells it back to check it. For your safety, the password is always typed, never spoken.

Writing pad

Open Writing pad from the menu. Type, or press Dictate and speak. Spelling mistakes are underlined by the browser. Press Read back to hear what you wrote, or Download to save it as a text file. Your draft is kept in this browser until you clear it.

Good to know

  • Listening to your voice works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. It needs the internet and permission to use the microphone.
  • Browsers do not let a website speak until you click or press a key once on the page.
  • Reading aloud uses your browser's own voice, so it can sound different on another computer.
